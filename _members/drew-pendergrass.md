Drew Pendergrass joined Oberlin College in Fall 2026 as Assistant Professor of Environmental Studies and Sciences. Previously he was a postdoctoral researcher at the Nicholas School of the Environment at Duke University. He completed both his Ph.D. in Environmental Engineering and his B.A. in Physics and Mathematics at Harvard University. Outside of the lab, Drew also produces scholarship in science and technology studies and ecological economics. For more information, visit his [home page](http://drewpendergrass.com).
