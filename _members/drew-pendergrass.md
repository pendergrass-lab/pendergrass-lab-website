Drew Pendergrass joined Oberlin College in Fall 2026 as Assistant Professor of Environmental Studies and Sciences. Previously he was a postdoctoral researcher at the Nicholas School of the Environment at Duke University. He completed both his Ph.D. in Environmental Engineering and his B.A. in Physics and Mathematics at Harvard University.
